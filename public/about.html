OUR PURPOSE

Connected by music. Rooted in Bono.

Bono Musicians Association exists to strengthen the professional community of musicians and entertainment practitioners in the Bono Region. The platform helps verified members present their work and receive event enquiries.

Membership administration is restricted to authorized association administrators and group coordinators. Public directory profiles appear only after association approval.
