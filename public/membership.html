BOMA membership is managed by association-approved administrators and group coordinators. There is no public self-registration.

HOW IT WORKS

Invitation first.
Community always.

01 An authorized BOMA coordinator creates your member invitation.

02 Use the secure, time-limited invitation link to verify your email and set a password.

03 Complete your profile and submit it for association review.

04 Once approved, you can access your member portal and verified directory profile.

If you are already a member, contact your group coordinator for an invitation. The association has not published general membership fees or application dates on this site.
